Delete Your Data

Sliceward has no accounts and no server of its own: your progress lives on your phone. Here is how to remove everything, at any time. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Your progress

Your progress is stored only on your phone. Uninstall the app to delete it: this removes your completed levels, best times, achievements earned in the app, your Prisms (including purchased ones) and your settings. On Android you can also clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Sliceward → Storage → Clear data). A copy may remain in your phone's own backup (Android Backup, iCloud) until that backup is replaced; you can delete it from your device's backup settings.

Achievements on Play Games or Game Center

Achievements you shared with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center belong to your player profile there. On Android, open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data and choose Sliceward (or your whole profile). On iPhone, Game Center data is managed by Apple: sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings to stop sharing, and contact Apple to delete it.

Ads, statistics and crash reports

Advertising data is held by Google AdMob and tied to your device's advertising identifier: reset or delete that identifier in your device settings, and change your ad choices in the app at Settings → Account → Privacy (ad choices). Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) and crash reports (Sentry) are tied to a random installation identifier (GameAnalytics also to the advertising identifier, where your device allows it), not to your name. If you want them deleted, email vitp.work@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request; we will ask both services to delete the data they can link to your installation and confirm within 30 days. Crash reports expire on their own after at most 90 days.

Purchases

The records of your purchases ("Remove ads" and Prism packs) are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies; the "Remove ads" record lets you restore it on a new phone. They do not contain your game progress. Prisms themselves are stored only on your phone and are deleted with the app.
