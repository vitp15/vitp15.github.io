Delete Your Data

Impossible Taxi has no account and no server of its own: there is no profile, e-mail address or name to delete. Everything the Application keeps about you lives on your device, and you can remove it yourself at any time. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Your progress on this device

Open Settings → Reset progress in the Application (tap twice to confirm). This deletes completed levels, unlocked taxi colours, achievements progress kept by the Application and your settings. Uninstalling the Application removes the same data. If your device restores a backup (Android Backup or iCloud), the progress saved in it comes back; delete the backup in your device settings to remove it too.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Change or withdraw your consent in Settings → Privacy options (where the law requires it). Reset or delete your device's advertising identifier in the device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Data that Google holds about ads is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) and crash reports (Sentry)

These receive a random installation identifier that is not linked to your name, e-mail or account, so the Service Provider cannot find "your" records on request. Uninstalling the Application ends the identifier; a new installation gets a new one. Statistics are kept by GameAnalytics and crash reports by Sentry for a limited period under their own policies (GameAnalytics, Sentry).

Achievements (Google Play Games / Game Center)

Achievements belong to your Google Play Games profile or your Game Center account, not to the Application. Manage or delete them there: on Android in the Google Play Games settings (delete your Play Games profile), on iOS in Settings → Game Center.

Purchases

The optional "Pro" purchase is recorded by Google Play or the Apple App Store, which keep receipts under their own policies for legal and accounting reasons. Deleting your data in the Application does not cancel or refund a purchase; you can restore Pro later from Settings → Restore purchase.
