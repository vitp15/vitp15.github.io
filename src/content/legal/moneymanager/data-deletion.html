Request Account and Data Deletion

If you want to delete your MoneyManager account and all associated data , please follow one of the methods below:

Method 1: In-App (Recommended)

Go to Settings > Delete all data within the mobile app. This erases the data on your phone and, if you turned on backup, sync or groups, the data stored for you on our server.

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to vitp.work@gmail.com. from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject: Account and Data Deletion Request.

Note: Once processed, your account and all data stored for you on our server (and, for users of older versions, your Firebase sign-in record and the data stored in Firestore) will be permanently deleted. Data that only lives on your phone is removed by uninstalling the app or with Delete all data. This action cannot be undone.
