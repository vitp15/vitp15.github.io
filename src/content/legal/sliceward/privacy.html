Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Sliceward app (hereinafter "Application") for iOS and Android mobile devices, developed by Vadim Plămădeală (hereinafter "Service Provider") as an ad-supported game with optional in-app purchases. This service is provided "AS IS". Last updated: 26 November 2026.

What the Application collects

Sliceward does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any contact details. Your progress (levels completed, best times, achievements earned, Prisms, settings) is stored only on your device and in your device's standard backup (Android Backup, iCloud), under the control of your operating system. The Service Provider runs no server that stores data about you.

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). Serves full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (the "look inside" help on a level and the daily Prism bonus). It may use your device's advertising identifier and your approximate location derived from the IP address. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland a consent form is shown before any ad is loaded; in US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information in the same form. On iPhone, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether ads may use the advertising identifier. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. See Google's privacy policy.
  • GameAnalytics (gameplay statistics). Receives events such as "level 12 started / completed / failed", the time taken, the screens and characters used, ads shown, hints, and which Prism pack was bought (never payment details), together with the device model, OS version, a random installation identifier and, where your device allows it, the advertising identifier, so that level difficulty and the game's balance can be tuned. It is started only after the consent step above. See GameAnalytics' privacy policy.
  • Sentry (crash reporting). If the Application crashes or hits an error, a technical report (device model, OS version, the code path) is sent so the bug can be fixed. Each session is also recorded (start, end, whether it crashed) so that the share of sessions without crashes can be measured. Sentry receives a random installation identifier, never your name, e-mail address or advertising identifier. See Sentry's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Games Services / Apple Game Center (achievements). If you are signed in to Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), the Application tells that service which achievements you have earned, linked to your player profile there. The Application itself does not receive your name or e-mail address. See Google's and Apple's privacy policies.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (the optional purchases: "Remove ads" and Prism packs). Payment is handled entirely by the store; the Application only learns which product was bought, never your payment details.

Network requests made by the Application

Besides the services above, the Application downloads a small public settings file from the Service Provider's website (ad timing, when the rating prompt and the offers may appear, the hint settings) and asks Google Play or Apple's App Store whether a newer version exists. Versions 1.1 and earlier on Android also read the latest version number from the same website. These requests contain no personal data.

If you write to us

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own e-mail app with a message to the Service Provider, pre-filled with the game version, the OS version, the device model and the language (you can delete them before sending). If you send it, we receive your e-mail address and your message and use them only to answer you.

Age

The Application is intended for people aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time: in the Application open Settings → Account → Privacy (ad choices). The option appears where the law requires a consent choice. Saying no does not remove ads; it only stops them being personalised.
  • On iPhone, you can change the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Remove all ads with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase; the analytics and crash services remain active.
  • Erase your progress by uninstalling the Application: it is stored only on your device. See Delete your data for everything else.

Retention

Progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Application. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. Gameplay statistics, advertising data and achievement records are kept by GameAnalytics, Google and Apple under their own policies.

Security

All connections use encryption in transit (HTTPS). The Service Provider relies on the security measures of the third-party services listed above and stores no server-side data about you.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Application after a change means you accept the updated policy.
