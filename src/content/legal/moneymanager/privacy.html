Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the MoneyManager app (hereinafter "Application") for Android and iOS, developed by Vadim Plămădeală (hereinafter "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. The Service Provider is responsible for the processing described here. This service is provided "AS IS". Last updated: 13 November 2026.


The short version

MoneyManager keeps your money data on your phone. You do not need an account to use it, and nothing about you is created on our server until you sign in. If you sign in and turn on backup and sync (PRO) or use a shared group, your data is stored on our server in the European Union, with names, amounts and notes encrypted on your phone before they leave it. The free version may show ads; PRO removes them. We never sell your data.


Accounts

The Application works without registration and without an account on our server. You may sign in with Google, with Apple or with your e-mail address (we e-mail you a one-time code; there is no password). Signing in lets you keep PRO on every phone, join shared groups and, with PRO, back up and sync your data. For a signed-in account we store your e-mail address (with Apple's "Hide My Email" this is a relay address), the sign-in method, the profile details Google or Apple share with us (such as your name), the account's creation date and the language of the Application, so that sign-in e-mails reach you in your language. On Android, signing in with Apple opens Apple's sign-in page in your browser.


What stays on your phone

Your accounts, transactions, budgets, categories, templates, loans, notes and settings are stored in a database on your device. It is excluded from the phone's own cloud backups (iCloud, Google backup); use backup and sync (PRO) or Settings > Export your data to keep a copy. The following also never leave your phone: the app lock PIN (stored only as a one-way hash), your fingerprint or Face ID (checked by the phone's system, never seen by the Application), the summaries shown by home-screen widgets, reminders and other local notifications, and the files you export. Uninstalling the Application removes the local data.


What information we collect, and when
  • Backup and sync (only when you sign in and have PRO): your accounts, categories, transactions, budgets, loans, templates and space settings are uploaded to our server (Supabase, European Union) so that they are backed up and the same on all your phones. Before upload, the Application encrypts on your phone (AES-256) the names of accounts, categories and budgets, budget descriptions, all amounts and exchange rates, notes, the names of the people in loans and the values of settings. To sort, sync and apply permissions, our server sees, unencrypted: dates, currencies, the type of each item (expense, income, transfer…), colours and icons, the order of lists, and which account, category or budget an item belongs to.
  • Encryption keys: each space (your personal data, each group) has its own key, created on your phone. By default a copy of your personal key is stored on our server, sealed with a separate server secret that is not kept in the database, so that signing in on a new phone restores your data. This means that the Service Provider could technically open your data; it does not do so. If you turn on Personal key (Settings), that copy is deleted and your key is locked with a password and a recovery code that only you know; then nobody else, including us, can open your data, and if you lose both, the data in your account cannot be recovered. On the phone the keys are kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore).
  • Shared groups (spaces): creating a group needs PRO; joining one needs only a signed-in account. A group's name and its members' display names are encrypted with the group's key; members' roles, colours and joining dates are not. Members see the group's accounts and budgets according to the permissions the group's administrators set (for example full, amounts only or private). The administrators decide who is invited and what each member may see or change. An invitation is a link or QR code that is valid for 7 days and can be revoked; its secret part (after the "#") never reaches our server. Entries you added stay in the group for its other members when you leave; the group's data is then removed from your phone. A member who is removed keeps what was already on their phone. If you delete your account, your place in your groups is removed and, if you were the only administrator, the role passes to another administrator or partner member; if there is none, the group becomes read-only. Nothing else in the group is deleted.
  • Group notifications: while you are signed in and a member of a group, the Application stores on our server your device's push token, its platform and language and whether you want "Group activity" notifications. When another member adds transactions, our server sends those who want them a generic notification ("new activity in the group", no names or amounts) through Firebase Cloud Messaging (and, on iPhone, Apple's push service). The token is deleted when you sign out.
  • Automatic capture of payments (optional): if you turn it on, the Application reads the notifications of the banking or payment apps you choose (Android) or the payment details passed by an iPhone Shortcuts automation you set up. This is done on your phone. Only if you are signed in with PRO and turn on "Ask AI", the title and text of a notification the Application cannot read on its own, or that names a shop it does not know yet, are sent to our server function and from there to Anthropic (Claude), which reads the amount, currency and merchant and picks one of your own categories and accounts. Before sending, the Application replaces card numbers, IBANs, phone and reference numbers, dates and times; it also sends the identifier of the app that posted the notification, your account's currency and the names of your categories and accounts in that space (never amounts or balances). For a payment passed by the iPhone Shortcuts automation (Apple Pay), the Application reads it on your phone and, with "Ask AI" on, sends only the name of a shop it does not know yet and the payment's amount and currency, with the names of your categories in that space, so that Anthropic (Claude) picks one of your categories; the card, the account names and your account's currency are never sent. Our server checks your PRO before any reading. We do not store the text; we keep only daily counters per user, for 7 days, to limit cost and abuse.
  • Feedback: if you send feedback, the message, its type, the optional e-mail address you add, the Application's language and, if you tick the box, your device model, operating system and version and the app version are stored on our server and e-mailed to the Service Provider. Feedback is not linked to your account.
  • Exchange rates: the Application downloads exchange rates from our server about once a day. Our server gets them from an exchange-rate provider (exchangeratesapi.io); nothing about you is sent to it.
  • Purchases: your PRO status is managed with RevenueCat on top of Google Play Billing or the App Store, under a random identifier and, once you sign in, your account's identifier. We do not see or store your payment card details.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: we use Firebase Crashlytics to learn about crashes (the error, the device model, operating system, app version, the screen that was open and an installation identifier) and Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which features are used: the screens opened, actions from a fixed list (for example a transaction, budget or account created, a PRO feature tapped, an onboarding step, the name of a built-in category chosen) and purchases with their price, together with an app instance identifier, the device type, operating system, app version and the country or region derived from the network address. They never receive what you type, your amounts or your notes. Analytics is set not to be used for advertising.
  • Remote settings and updates: Firebase Remote Config lets us switch some features on or off without an update. The Application asks Google Play or the App Store whether a newer version exists and may ask you to rate it through their own review prompt.
  • Advertising (free version): Google AdMob may show ads and may use your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions that require it, a consent form is shown before any ad. On iPhone, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt then asks whether your advertising identifier may be used for personalised ads; if you decline you see non-personalised ads. You can change your choices any time in Settings > Privacy options and in the phone's settings. Your amounts, accounts and notes are never used for ads. PRO removes the ads.
  • Location, contacts, photos: the Application does not ask for your location, does not read your contacts and does not browse your photos; when you restore a backup it reads only the file you choose.

Moving from the older version of MoneyManager

If you used the first version of MoneyManager with an account, your data was stored in Google Firebase. So that you do not have to start again, the Service Provider copies it once, on its side, from Firebase to our server in the European Union, encrypted in the same way as described above, together with your sign-in details (e-mail address, Firebase user identifier and, for e-mail accounts, the one-way hash of your password, never the password itself) and your PRO status. When you open the new version on a phone where the older version was signed in, the Application uses that sign-in to connect you to the copied data. The data in Firebase is kept only while the older version is still in use (at most about 12 months after the new version is released) and is then deleted. If you delete your account in the new version, we keep only a one-way hash of your e-mail address and of your old user identifier, so that the copy is not created again.


Does the Application use Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Only in the optional "Ask AI" feature described above, which is available to signed-in PRO users who turn it on. It reads the amount, currency and merchant from a payment notification and picks one of your own categories and accounts by name; for an Apple Pay payment on iPhone it only picks one of your categories from the shop's name and the amount. Anthropic processes the text only to return that reading, under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this data. AI is not used to profile you or to show ads, and MoneyManager does not give financial advice.

Your rights and how to opt out

You can use the Application without any data on our server by not signing in and not using "Ask AI" or feedback. You can turn sync, group notifications, automatic capture and "Ask AI" off in Settings, withdraw the advertising consent in Settings > Privacy options, sign out, delete your account and uninstall the Application at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom you also have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Use Settings > Export your data to get a copy, or contact us.


Data retention and deletion
  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it: Settings > Delete all data erases everything on the phone (it does not delete your account).
  • Data on our server (account, backup, keys, group membership) stays until you delete your account: Settings > Delete account (shown when you are signed in, also under Settings > Account and backup) or by e-mail; see the deletion page. Entries you added to a group stay in that group for its other members. A subscription is cancelled in Google Play or the App Store; deleting the account does not cancel it.
  • Items you delete are kept on the server as deletion markers for 90 days, so that your other phones remove them too; deleted spaces are erased after 90 days.
  • The encrypted backups of our server database are made weekly and kept for 35 days.
  • Feedback messages are kept until they are handled and are deleted on request. Abuse-limiting counters are kept for 7 days.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days and analytics data according to the Firebase retention setting (14 months at most). Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as their rules and the law require.
  • The older version's data in Firebase: see "Moving from the older version of MoneyManager" above.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact the Service Provider (vitp.work@gmail.com) so that it can be deleted.


How is your information kept secure?

Data sent to or from our server travels over encrypted connections (TLS). Names, amounts and notes are encrypted on your phone before upload, as described above, and the server only accepts them in encrypted form. Access to the server is limited to what is needed to run the service. You can lock the Application with a PIN, fingerprint or Face ID. No system can prevent every breach, but we work to provide reasonable protection.


Purchases and payments

PRO is bought through Google Play Billing or the App Store, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment card details go directly to Google or Apple and are never seen by us. RevenueCat receives the purchase status so that PRO works on all your devices.


International transfers

Our server is in the European Union. Some providers (for example Anthropic, Google, Apple, GitHub, RevenueCat, Resend and Brevo) may process data outside the European Economic Area under standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.
