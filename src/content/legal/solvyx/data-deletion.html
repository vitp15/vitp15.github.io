Request Account and Data Deletion

Solvyx can be played without signing in. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your device, but not the online data of your guest account: when your device is online, the app sends each solved level to the global leaderboard under a guest account it creates automatically, with your approximate country and any display name you chose. To delete a guest account or an account you created (via email, Google, or Apple), and all associated data, use one of the methods below:

Method 1: In-App (Recommended)

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account and Data within the mobile app. If you play as a guest, the button appears once you save the account on the same screen; or use Method 2.

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to vitp.work@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account (for a guest account, tell us your display name). Use the subject: Account and Data Deletion Request.

Note: Once processed, your account and all associated data (your profile and display name, leaderboard scores and stat values, daily results, duels, friends, blocked players, the reports you made, push notification tokens, and coin balance) will be permanently deleted from our servers. This action cannot be undone. The suggestions and problem reports you sent from Settings → Feedback are kept without any link to your account.

We confirm and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. If you signed in with Google or Apple (including Apple's Private Relay @privaterelay.appleid.com address) and cannot email us from the account address, include your display name and any details that help us locate your account. Some information may be retained where required by law (for example, transaction records for tax or fraud-prevention purposes) and is deleted once that legal retention period ends.
