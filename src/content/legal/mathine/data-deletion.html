Delete Your Data

Mathine has no sign-up: it creates an anonymous account on its server for shared worlds and verification of online Pro access. Here is how to remove everything, at any time. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

From the app

Open Together → Account and server data → Delete my server data and confirm. This deletes, immediately:

  • your anonymous account and its secret key;
  • the connection between that account and its store purchase proof; proofs with no remaining associated account are removed;
  • your membership in the shared worlds you joined;
  • the shared worlds you own. You can transfer ownership to another member from the world management screen before deleting your account; otherwise, the world is deleted for everyone and its members are told.

Your factory on the phone stays until you uninstall the app (on Android you can also clear the app's storage: Settings → Apps → Mathine → Storage → Clear data). A copy may remain in your phone's own backup (Android Backup, iCloud) until that backup is replaced.

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, email vitp.work@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. Include the generated player identifier shown in Together → Account and server data, if available. Do not email your private recovery key. We will arrange a suitable verification step and respond within 30 days. Because accounts are anonymous, device model and an approximate date alone may not let us identify your records safely.

Achievements on Play Games or Game Center

Achievements you shared with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center belong to your player profile there. On Android, open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data and choose Mathine (or your whole profile). On iPhone and iPad, Game Center data is managed by Apple: sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings to stop sharing, and contact Apple to delete it.

Ads, statistics and crash reports

Advertising data is held by Google AdMob and tied to your device's advertising identifier: reset or delete that identifier in your device settings, and change your ad choices in the app at Settings → Privacy (ad choices). Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) and crash reports (Sentry) are tied to a random installation identifier, not to your name. To have them deleted, email us with the subject above; we will ask both services to delete the data they can link to your installation where identification is possible and respond within 30 days. You can stop further optional analytics and error reporting from the separate switches in Settings.

Purchases

The record of the Pro purchase is kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies (it lets you restore the purchase on a new phone). It does not contain your game data.
