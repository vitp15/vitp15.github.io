Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Mathine app (listed in the stores as "Mathine: Math Factory Puzzle", hereinafter "Application") for iOS and Android mobile devices, developed by Vadim Plămădeală (hereinafter "Service Provider") as an ad-supported game with an optional one-time purchase called Pro. This service is provided "AS IS". Last updated: 26 October 2026.

What the Application collects

Mathine does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or any contact details, and it has no chat. Your factory, progress and settings are stored on your device.

To let you build together with friends and verify access to online Pro features, the Application creates an anonymous account on the Service Provider's server the first time it connects: a random identifier and a secret key, kept in the Application. The server stores, linked to that identifier:

  • your shared factories, progress, upgrades and statistics; personal single-player factories are not uploaded;
  • the shared worlds you create or join with an invite code: their factory, the building actions of every member and a generated player name (for example "Engineer 4821"). Members of a shared world see each other's generated names and constructions; nothing else about you is shown to them;
  • store purchase tokens or Apple-signed transaction proofs and their verification status, used to restore and validate Pro access to online features. Payment details are not sent to the game server;

The server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which processes this data on the Service Provider's behalf. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The Application also uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). Serves banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads (for example a short production boost you choose to watch an ad for). It may use your device's advertising identifier and your approximate location derived from the IP address. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the advertising consent flow is resolved before any ad is loaded; in US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information in the same form. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether ads may use the advertising identifier. Advertising availability depends on the choices and permissions you give. Declining tracking does not block gameplay. See Google's privacy policy.
  • GameAnalytics (optional gameplay statistics). When you enable Usage statistics in Settings, it receives fixed gameplay events such as a level starting or being completed and a reward being earned, session information, device/app information and an installation identifier. Player circuit names, invitation codes and account recovery keys are not included. This setting is off by default and can be disabled at any time. See GameAnalytics' privacy policy.
  • Sentry (crash reporting). With Error reports enabled in Settings, technical reports such as the device model, OS and app versions, error type and code path help diagnose failures. Screenshots, save files, game logs and script variable values are not attached. You can turn this off at any time. Network providers may process your IP address. See Sentry's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Games Services / Apple Game Center (achievements). If you are signed in to Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone, iPad), the Application tells that service which achievements you earned, linked to your player profile there. The Application itself does not receive your name or e-mail address. See Google's and Apple's privacy policies.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (the optional Pro purchase). Payment is handled entirely by the store. The Application receives the product, localized price and purchase status, and sends a purchase proof to the game server for verification with Google or Apple. It never receives card details.

Network requests made by the Application

Besides the services above, the Application downloads a small public configuration file from the Service Provider's website (advertising settings). No account identifier is included in that request, but the website host processes ordinary connection information such as IP addresses.

Age

The Application is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the Application, write to the address below and the related server data will be deleted.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time: in the Application open Settings → Privacy (ad choices). The option appears where the law requires a consent choice. Saying no does not remove ads; it only stops them being personalised.
  • On iPhone and iPad, change the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Remove advertising with Pro. Analytics and error reporting remain controlled by their separate settings.
  • Enable or disable an optional daily reminder in Settings and choose its time. Reminders are local device notifications, with permission requested only when you enable them. Your chosen time and completion calendar are stored on your device; no push-notification service receives them.
  • Delete everything the server holds about you from the Application: Together → Account and server data → Delete my server data. See Delete your data for all the options, including without the app.

Retention

Anonymous accounts, memberships and shared factories remain until you delete them. When an owner deletes a shared world, it is removed for every member. Purchase proofs are removed when no remaining server account uses them; store purchase records remain with Google or Apple. Technical rate-limiting records and temporary connection tickets expire automatically. Gameplay statistics, advertising data, error reports and achievement records are retained by their respective providers under their policies and the configured service retention.

Security

All connections use encryption in transit (HTTPS / secure WebSockets). Only members of a shared world can join it, the secret key of your anonymous account never leaves your device and the server, and administrative service credentials and store-verification keys are kept on the server, not in the Application.
