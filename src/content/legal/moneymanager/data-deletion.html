Request Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your MoneyManager account and the data stored for it on our server at any time, in the app or by e-mail.

Method 1: In the app (recommended)

Open Settings > Delete account (the row is shown while you are signed in; it is also under Settings > Account and backup) and confirm. This deletes, on our server: your account and sign-in details, your backup and synced data, your encryption keys, the PRO status tied to the account, your devices' notification tokens and your place in shared groups. You then choose whether the data on the phone stays (without an account) or is erased too. A subscription is cancelled in Google Play or the App Store; deleting the account does not cancel it.

Settings > Delete all data erases everything on the phone only; it does not delete your account. If you never signed in, your data lives only on the phone, and this (or uninstalling the app) removes it.

Method 2: E-mail request

If you can no longer open the app, send an e-mail to vitp.work@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject Account and Data Deletion Request. We reply to confirm and delete the same data as Method 1.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Entries you added to a shared group stay in that group for its other members. If you were the group's only administrator, the role passes to another administrator or partner member; if there is none, the group becomes read-only.
  • Our encrypted database backups are overwritten within 35 days.
  • If you used the first version of MoneyManager, its copy of your data in Google Firebase is deleted when that version is retired (at most about 12 months after the new version is released), or earlier if you ask by e-mail. We keep a one-way hash of your e-mail address and of your old user identifier, so that the old data is not copied to your deleted account again.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as their rules and the law require. Feedback messages are not linked to your account; ask by e-mail to have yours deleted.

This action cannot be undone.
