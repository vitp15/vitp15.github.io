Huglet

Draw something. It lands on their home screen.

Huglet is a small app for two people. Send a drawing or a note to your person's home-screen widget, send a touch they can feel and, on Android, change their wallpaper — always with their permission, and only for adults (18+).

The person receiving is in control. They decide what you may send, set daily limits and quiet hours, and can pause or block at any time.

On iPhone, apps cannot change the wallpaper, and custom touches play when Huglet is opened. Huglet says so in the app.
