Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is applicable to the Solvyx app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by Vadim Plămădeală (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?
What information do users provide to the Application?

Solvyx works offline and does not require an account. Your game progress, puzzle statistics, and coin balance are stored locally on your device. You can play without providing any personal information.

Some optional features — global leaderboards, asynchronous duels, and syncing your progress across devices — use an online account. By default this account is anonymous: it is a random identifier with no personal information attached. If you choose to save your progress permanently, you may create an account using your email address, which is verified with a one-time code sent to that address — the Application never asks you for a password — or by signing in with Google or Apple. You may also choose a display name that is shown on leaderboards. Registration is not mandatory; however, some online features are unavailable unless you create or sign in to an account.

When you use these online features, the Service Provider stores your account information and game data on its servers — such as your leaderboard scores and stat values, duel results, coin balance, any display name you choose, the friend connections you create, and the contents of any report or suggestion you send from within the Application (its category and message, along with the app version and language, so the report can be acted on). The Service Provider may also use the contact information you provide to send you important information, required notices, and — where permitted — occasional marketing.

If you allow it, the Application may send push notifications — for example when an opponent finishes a duel you are playing, or to remind you about the daily challenge. Delivering these requires a device notification token, which is stored with your account and is used for nothing else. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings, which stops the notifications without affecting the rest of the Application.


What information does the Application collect automatically?

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device's unique identifiers (such as the device ID and advertising identifier), the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, and information about the way you use the Application. The IP address is also used to determine an approximate country, which is shown next to leaderboard entries.


Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device. It does not request or use GPS or precise-location permissions. As described above, an approximate country is derived from your IP address for leaderboard purposes only.


Does the Application use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies?

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features. Puzzles are generated by a deterministic algorithm on your device.

California residents also have the right to know, delete, correct, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights; to exercise them, contact vitp.work@gmail.com. The Service Provider retains your account and game data (leaderboard scores, stat values, duel results, coin balance, display name) for as long as your account exists. Automatically collected diagnostic and analytics information is retained for up to 24 months and thereafter may be kept only in aggregated form. Local data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Application.

The Application may display advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: Before personalized ads are served, the Application shows a consent message (using a Google-certified consent management platform) so you can grant or refuse consent for the use of your data for advertising, and you can update your choice later. Where consent is refused, only non-personalized ads are shown.

On Apple devices, any use of the advertising identifier for this purpose is subject to the App Tracking Transparency permission described under "Advertising" above.
