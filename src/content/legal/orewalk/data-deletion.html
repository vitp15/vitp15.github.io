Request Account and Data Deletion

Orewalk creates an anonymous game account on first launch. If you never linked it to Google or Apple, uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone, and the anonymous account on our servers holds no personal information. To delete your game account and all its data from our servers, use one of the methods below.

Method 1: In the app (recommended)

Open Settings in Orewalk and choose Delete account.

Method 2: Email request

Send an email to vitp.work@gmail.com with the subject Orewalk Account and Data Deletion Request. Include your player code or public game name, and send it from the email address of the Google or Apple account linked to the game if you can. If you used Sign in with Apple with a private relay address (@privaterelay.appleid.com), include any details that help us find your account.

What is deleted: your profile and game name, your cells and walk summaries, your game progress and save copy, your friendships, your inbox, any restrictions on your account, and the reports made against you. Market sales stay in the other player's history without your name, and reports you made stay without your name. This cannot be undone.

We complete verified requests within 30 days. Some information may be kept longer only where the law requires it (for example purchase records for tax purposes) and is deleted when that period ends. Purchases themselves are recorded by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies.
