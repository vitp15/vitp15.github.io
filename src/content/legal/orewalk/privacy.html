Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Orewalk app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by Vadim Plămădeală (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Orewalk is a walking game on the real map: you start an "expedition", walk outdoors, and the map cells you walk through become your territory for a limited time. This policy explains what that means for your data, and in particular for your location.


Your account

The first time you open the Application, it creates an anonymous game account: a random identifier with no personal information attached. You can play without giving any personal information. If you choose to keep your progress across devices, or to use the market and friends, you can link this account to Google or to Apple (Sign in with Apple). The Service Provider then receives the account identifier and the email address that Google or Apple shares (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address). The Application never asks for a password.

You choose a public game name (a pseudonym) and an avatar from the game. Please do not use your real name. Names are checked against a list of banned words.


Location

The Application uses your device's precise location (GPS) only during an expedition that you start yourself, and only to count the cells you walk through. The expedition keeps running with the screen off, with the system location indicator visible, and stops when you stop it in the Application. The Application does not use location at any other time and never asks for "Always" (background) location access.

Your exact coordinates and your route stay on your device for a short time and are then deleted. They are not sent to our servers. When you claim territory, the Application sends only:

  • the identifiers of the cells you walked through (each cell is a map hexagon of about 0.1 km²);
  • a summary of the walk: its length, its duration, the highest speed, and whether the location looked simulated.

The summary is used to keep the game fair (for example, territory is not counted from a car) and to count your walking for the game.

Your location is never shared with advertising or analytics providers. Those providers may still estimate a general area (for example a city) from your device's IP address, as any internet service can.


What other players can see

Orewalk is designed so that nobody can follow where you walk:

  • on the map, other players see only how many players hold each cell, not who they are, and only with a delay of several hours;
  • your public profile shows your game name, avatar, level, titles and leaderboard scores, from a snapshot taken every 30 minutes. It does not show your cells, your home area or your walks;
  • your cells are shown only to mutual friends (players who added each other), also with a delay of several hours. Anonymous mode hides them from friends too, and hides your name on your market listings;
  • nobody sees your live position, your routes, the exact time of your walks, a photo of you or your real name.

Game data on our servers

To run the shared world (territory, market, friends and leaderboards), the Service Provider stores with your account:

  • your game progress, coins and resources, and a copy of your save;
  • the cells you hold, and the walk summaries described above;
  • your market listings and sales, and your in-game inbox;
  • the players you added as friends and the players you blocked;
  • reports you send about another player (the reason and an optional note), and any warnings or restrictions on your own account, with their reason and dates;
  • the status of your purchases (handled by RevenueCat, see "Purchases").

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), your achievements and leaderboard scores are also sent to that service, where its own privacy policy applies.

Reminders about your cells (for example before a cell's time runs out) are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the Application or in your device settings.


What the Application collects automatically

The Application may automatically collect the type of device, the operating system version, device and app-instance identifiers (including, for advertising, the advertising identifier), the IP address, crash and diagnostic information, and information about how the Application is used.


Analytics and crash reports

The Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example how far players get on their first day, or how often a rewarded ad is watched). It sends a small set of game events with simple values such as a level or a count, and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. It sends no name, no account identifier, no coordinates and no cells, and it does not use the advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Moldova, analytics stays off unless you agree to it. It is never used for advertising.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (stack traces, device and operating system details and the app state at the time of the crash) so the Service Provider can fix bugs. Firebase Remote Config lets the Service Provider adjust game values, such as how often ads may appear, without an update; it reads your country, language and device version.


Advertising

The Application shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game reward. Other ad formats may be enabled later. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and your interactions with ads to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads, and to prevent fraud.

Apple devices (iOS): before any tracking that uses your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), the Application asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you are not tracked; you may still see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: before personalized ads are shown, the Application displays a consent message (Google's certified consent platform), so you can accept or refuse the use of your data for advertising and change your choice later. If you refuse, only non-personalized ads are shown.

You can also reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.


Purchases and payments

If you buy something in Orewalk, payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and purchases are managed through RevenueCat. The Service Provider never receives or stores your payment card details. Coins bought with real money cannot be traded between players.


The map

Map tiles are downloaded from OpenFreeMap (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). Like any website, the tile server receives your IP address and the area of the map being displayed.


Does the Application use Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

No. The Application does not use AI technologies to process your data. The game's world and resources are generated from public map and geology data.


Who processes your data

The Service Provider uses the following providers to run the Application. They process data on the Service Provider's behalf, except the advertising providers, which also act for their own purposes as described in their policies. The Service Provider does not sell your personal information.

  • Supabase: the game server and database (hosted in the European Union, Frankfurt)
  • Cloudflare: delivery of public leaderboards and map counts
  • Google: Google Play Services, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google Play Games, Sign in with Google
  • Google AdMob: advertising
  • RevenueCat: purchase management
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Game Center and App Store purchases (iOS)
  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles

The Service Provider may also disclose information when required by law, or when it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, your safety or the safety of others, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a government request.


Legal bases (EEA, UK, Moldova)

The Service Provider processes your data to perform the contract with you (to run the game, your account, the shared world and your purchases); with your consent (location during an expedition, personalized ads, tracking on iOS and, in the regions listed above, analytics), which you can withdraw at any time; for its legitimate interests (to keep the game fair and secure, to prevent cheating and fraud, to fix crashes and to improve the game); and to comply with legal obligations. You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.


Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account and its data at any time from the Application's settings, or as described on the Delete Account and Data page. California residents: the Service Provider does not sell personal information for money; using the advertising identifier for personalized ads may count as "sharing" under California law, and you can opt out by declining tracking on iOS, refusing personalized-ads consent, or turning off ad personalization in your device settings. Where technically supported, the Service Provider honors opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control.


How long data is kept
  • your account and game data: while your account exists;
  • daily walking totals: 30 days; walks flagged as suspicious: 90 days;
  • market sales: 1 year (after you delete your account, a sale stays in the other player's history without your name);
  • undelivered inbox items: 60 days; closed reports: 1 year;
  • analytics data: as long as Google Analytics keeps event data (at most 14 months), then only in aggregate; crash reports: 90 days.

When you delete your account, your profile, cells, friendships, inbox, restrictions and the reports made against you are deleted from our servers, normally within 30 days, except where the law requires keeping something longer. Local data is removed when you uninstall the Application.


International data transfers

The game server is in the European Union. Some providers (for example Google, RevenueCat and Cloudflare) may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Transfers out of the EEA or the UK are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.


Children

Orewalk is for people aged 16 and over. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact the Service Provider and it will be deleted.


Security

All data is encrypted in transit. Access to the server data is limited to what each part of the game needs, and other players never receive the identities on a cell, only counts. No security system can prevent every breach, but the Service Provider takes reasonable physical, electronic and procedural measures to protect your data.
